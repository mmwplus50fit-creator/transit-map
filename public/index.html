TRANSIT MAP / FIRST EDITION

A window onto Toronto.

Explore buses and streetcars using TTC BusTime positions and its matching surface schedule. Subway trains are not included.

When connected, positions refresh every 30 seconds while this page is visible. Reports older than 3 minutes are marked as older; positions older than 15 minutes are hidden in live mode. An unknown report time is never treated as fresh.

The preview uses an actual captured TTC report, clearly labelled as a snapshot. It does not simulate movement or arrival times.

Favourites stay on this device. Location is optional and used in your browser only. No account or AI service is needed.
